Your data in PROS

Your device saves the work you choose to record. An approved account can synchronize it for use on another device.

On your device Account and sync Olive and downloads Backups and removal

Saved in this browser

PROS stores your profile, skills, activities, projects, tasks, books, notes, journal entries, and preferences. Records and unfinished drafts save in browser storage. The sample world has separate records.

Browser storage can be cleared or evicted. Keep an external backup of work you want to protect.

What synchronizes

Firebase Authentication manages sign-in. Cloud Firestore stores synchronized records for approved accounts, including journal entries. Records are separated by account identity. Authorized project operators can administer account data. Synchronization does not provide end-to-end encryption.

Passwords and sign-in tokens are excluded from PROS backups. Hosting and account providers can process the requests needed to serve and secure their services. PROS has no advertising or analytics SDK configured.

Olive and optional downloads

Olive’s written guide, weekly recap, and activity interpretation run on your device. The optional local model processes a next-step prompt in your browser, using selected project and skill context. Its prompt excludes journal text, account details, and full history.

Model and runtime downloads contact Hugging Face and MLC AI’s GitHub distribution. These services receive download requests. The model cache is shared by profiles in the same browser and can be cleared separately.

Saved activity records can synchronize with an account. Raw interpretation text stays in its unfinished draft and can appear in a backup. Essential artwork caches with the app after setup. Audio plays from hosted files, and selected recordings can be kept offline.

Backups, sign-out, and removal

Backups are readable files that can include journal text and drafts. Keep them somewhere you trust. The app keeps seven local recovery snapshots for ordinary mistakes. An external copy helps if you lose the device or its browser data.

Sign-out keeps pending work in its original account unless you choose to remove the device copy. Account deletion requires an online connection and password confirmation. Incomplete removal can be resumed. A content-free marker may remain to keep the removed account frozen.

Copies on other devices, your backup files, and provider operational logs have their own retention and removal processes.

Questions about your records

Use the channel where you received your invitation for private account questions. The support guide lists the details to share when reporting a problem.
