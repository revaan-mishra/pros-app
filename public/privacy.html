YOUR RECORDS, YOUR CHOICES

Your data and privacy.

PROS stores the work you choose to record: your profile, skills, activities, projects, tasks, books, notes, journal entries and preferences. This page describes the current app’s data handling.

Saved on your device first

Records and unfinished drafts are stored in this browser. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted, so keep an external backup of work you want to protect. The separate sample world never contributes to your personal progress.

Private accounts and synchronization

For account users, Firebase Authentication manages sign-in, and Cloud Firestore stores synchronized app records, including journal text. Account records are separated by user identity. Firebase is a Google service; authorized project operators can administer its data. This is not end-to-end encrypted storage. Passwords and sign-in tokens are excluded from PROS backups.

Olive and activity interpretation

Olive’s written practice guidance, activity interpretation and factual weekly recap run on your device. They do not call a remote language model or send journal writing to an AI service. Your saved activity records still synchronize if you use an account; raw interpretation text stays in its unfinished draft and can appear in an exported backup.

Sound, artwork and browser requests

Essential artwork is packaged with the app. Optional recordings download only when you choose to keep them offline; playback uses the app’s hosted files. This app has no advertising or analytics SDK configured. Hosting and account providers can process the technical requests needed to serve and secure their services.

Backups and removal

Backup files are readable JSON and can include private writing and unfinished drafts. Keep them somewhere you trust. Seven local recovery snapshots help with mistakes but do not replace an external copy.

Sign-out preserves pending work unless you choose to remove the device copy. Account deletion requires an online connection, password confirmation and removal of synchronized records. Interrupted removal can be resumed. A content-free marker may remain to keep the removed account frozen. Copies already downloaded to another device, user-saved backup files and provider-managed operational logs require separate handling; account removal is not a remote wipe of your devices.

Questions or corrections

During private access, contact the project owner through the channel where you received your invitation. Do not send passwords, tokens or an unredacted journal backup. The support guide explains useful diagnostic details.
