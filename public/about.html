Where does your work belong?

Six places, each with its own work to keep and three restorations to discover. Follow an existing path or name a skill of your own.

  • Observatory

    Read deeply, practice a language, or find an answer you can explain.

  • Foundry

    Build a useful tool, repair a thing, or test an idea at the workbench.

  • Atelier

    Finish a take, a drawing, or a scene you can read from start to end.

  • Training Grounds

    Work on a movement and record the moment you can do it cleanly.

  • Citadel

    Practice a conversation, think through a decision, or rehearse a talk.

  • Sanctuary

    Make time for focus, reflection, and routines worth returning to.

Know where to pick up.

Leave a place to start
Pin your project to Today. Open the timer or record a session, then leave yourself a line about where to resume.
Keep the work you can point to
Put finished results on your shelf as keepsakes. The Chronicle brings together what you practiced and what you made.
Return whenever you’re ready
Your work saves on this device first. After setup, you can reopen it offline. A week away leaves your world where you left it.
