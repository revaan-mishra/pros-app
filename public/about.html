PERSONAL RPG OPERATING SYSTEM

A little effort.
A world of possibility.

Practice a skill. Finish something meaningful. Watch a living, illustrated world grow from the genuine things you do.

Install PROS Open in browser See how it works ↓

Standalone, local-first personal software. Zero streak decay. No ads. No telemetry.

Your life is not a spreadsheet. PROS turns the honest minutes you spend doing creative, physical, or intellectual work into character experience ($1\text{ min} = 1\text{ XP}$) and world restoration.

ONE CONNECTED JOURNEY

Choose. Practice. Keep something real.

01 / BEGIN

Give today a shape.

Choose a project and its next action. Record real effort, or review suggested fields. Focus keeps your place through interruptions without punishing you.

02 / DEVELOP

Follow your curiosity.

Explore 74 catalog skills or author your own. Levels reflect actual recorded time; milestones celebrate what you can show to the world.

03 / RESTORE

Leave an indelible mark.

Practice and completed outputs restore six illustrated regions. Display keepsakes linked to what you made, and revisit your journey in the Chronicle.

THE REALM NAVIGATOR

Meet Olive.

A quiet, faithful companion. Olive lives inside PROS to offer on-device guidance, gentle next-step orientation, and weekly reflection. Your journal entries never leave your device to generate suggestions.

Your pace. Your atmosphere.

Choose procedural melodies, natural soundscapes, or silence. Explore the world when you want to; keep the daily view minimal when you need to focus. Time away never damages your character or erases cumulative progress.

Your work saves on this device first in IndexedDB. Private account sync, correction review, Undo, and 7 rolling recovery snapshots ensure your records remain forever under your sovereignty.
