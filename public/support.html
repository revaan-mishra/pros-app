A CLEAR WAY FORWARD

Find your way around.

Where should I start?

Open Today, choose a current project, then its next action or Log activity. Record actual time and review the result. The optional three-stop tour and longer reference guide are in Help.

How do I install PROS?

On an authorized HTTPS app address, open Safari on iPhone, choose Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Mac, use your browser’s installation or bookmark option. Wait for Ready offline before relying on an offline launch. A Mac’s localhost address is not accessible from a phone.

What happens when I am offline?

An initialized account opens its saved local work. New changes wait on this device until you reconnect and synchronization is authorized. Initial account setup and a new device’s first restoration require a connection. Check Settings → Data and recovery for the current state.

Why can’t I open a new account?

Verify your email and confirm that your account has been enabled for private access. If you forgot your password, use the reset option in account entry. Never share your password with the project owner.

Does Olive use online AI?

Olive combines written guidance with the selected project and factual activity totals on your device. No remote AI model is connected. Her suggestions do not log work, complete tasks or grant XP.

How do I fix a record?

Open its activity or output history, review the correction and save. Progress and eligible rewards are recalculated together. Undo is available for supported changes. Conflicting device edits remain available for explicit review.

How do I protect my world?

Open Settings → Data and recovery → Export backup. Keep the file somewhere safe; it includes private writing. Imports validate first, preserve stable records and ask you to review conflicts. Export a draft before discarding it if it cannot open.

Why is there no sound or 3D?

Sound starts only when you choose Play. Save your listening choices deliberately. Optional recordings need their own download for offline use. Character’s wayfinder has a static illustration when the 3D view is unavailable, plus buttons and keyboard controls. Reduced motion suppresses continuous turning.

Will taking a break hurt my progress?

No. Absences cause no XP decay, damage or equipment loss. Overdue tasks can be rescheduled or paused. Correcting the actual evidence can change eligibility; simply being away cannot.

Report a problem

Contact the project owner through your invitation channel. Include the screen, action, exact message, browser/device and whether you were offline. Describe whether the result was saved, waiting to sync or failed. Share only the minimum necessary details; never send passwords, tokens or private backups without first reviewing their contents.
