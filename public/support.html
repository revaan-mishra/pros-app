Install and sign in

How do I put PROS on my phone?

Open the install guide for your device. You can use it before signing in.

  • Android. In Chrome, use Install on this device if offered. In Chrome’s menu, look for Install app or Install and create shortcut.
  • iPhone or iPad. In Safari, open Share → Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.

Open the PROS icon while online. Try the sample, or sign in with a verified email. Look for Ready offline in More → Install PROS.

My email is verified. Why can’t I open my world?

After following the email link, return to PROS and choose I’ve verified my email, then Open my world. If an older installation mentions an invitation, update PROS and try again.

I forgot my password

Choose Forgot password? from the sign-in screen. Follow the email link, then return to PROS and sign in again. Check your spam folder if the email hasn’t arrived.

Is there an Android download?

Download the signed APK from GitHub Releases and open it on your Android phone. Sign in with the account you use on the web to restore your world. The release page has the checksum and installation steps.

Work offline and keep a backup

What can I do without a connection?

After one online setup, you can reopen an initialized world and log work offline. Changes wait on that device until there’s a connection. Check Settings → Data and recovery for your sync status. Set up a new account or restore on a new phone while online.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

For a private account, let the old device finish syncing, then sign in to the same account on your new phone. If your progress is in a local profile, export a backup on the old device and review the import on the new one.

How do I save or restore a backup?

Open Settings → Data and recovery → Export backup. Save the file somewhere you trust, since it can include private writing and unfinished work. Use Import backup to review and restore it.

Can I fix a session I recorded incorrectly?

Find it in Activity history under More. Open the activity, make your correction, and review what will change before saving. Use Undo when shown to reverse the save.

Using PROS

Where do I start?

Choose Explore a sample world. Try a short Log activity entry from Today, then visit World to see where it counted. Your sample records stay separate from your private world.

What can Olive do?

Olive can offer a smaller next step or a factual recap. Her written guide and the activity sentence field work on your device. The optional on-device AI model needs a compatible browser and a roughly 220 MB download.

Where are the sound and motion controls?

Open the soundtrack library from More to preview music or nature sounds. Sound starts off. Change motion and appearance in Settings → Preferences. If 3D is unavailable, the wayfinder shows an illustration.

What happens if I take a break?

Your skills, wardrobe and world keep their progress. You can reopen an unfinished project, pause a routine, or move a due date when you return.

Report a problem

For a public bug report, include the screen, the action you took, the message you saw, and your browser or phone. Keep passwords, notes, backups, and account details private.

For a vulnerability, use the private reporting instructions in the security policy.
