# PROS product and launch kit

Prepared 17 November 2026. This is reusable product copy and launch material, not an announcement that PROS is public. The [implementation checkpoint](IMPLEMENTATION-CHECKPOINT.md) controls readiness. Native App Store copy is a separate draft for a future qualifying native release.

## Positioning

**Name:** PROS  
**Tagline:** A world of your making.  
**Promise:** Give real effort a place to live.  
**Category:** Personal RPG, skill practice and project companion.  
**Audience:** People who want structure and a sense of adventure around their own learning, creative work and everyday projects.

**One sentence:** PROS turns skill practice and finished projects into the restoration of an illustrated world, with a calm daily interface and no lost progress for taking time away.

**Short pitch:** Practice guitar. Build a useful tool. Finish a book. In PROS, the things you actually do develop your character and restore six places in a shared personal world. Projects become expeditions, finished outputs become keepsakes, and Olive, your white Spitz companion, helps you find a smaller next step. Your work saves locally first, and you stay in control of corrections, backups and sound.

**Long pitch:** A list can tell you what remains. PROS also gives you somewhere to keep what you have made. Choose a project, practice a skill and confirm a real result. The Observatory lights up with knowledge; the Foundry comes alive with working ideas; the Atelier makes room for finished work. Six regions develop through relevant effort and meaningful outputs, using the same evidence that powers your skills and character.

Everyday use stays straightforward: one next action on Today, quick activity capture, a focus place and clear requirements. The richer world is there when you want to explore it. No character damage follows an absence. No checkbox invents practice time. Olive offers optional guidance on your device, and the Chronicle connects your real activity with the things worth remembering. PROS makes room for returning, revising and beginning again.

## What sets it apart

| Product choice | Concrete experience | Safe claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Effort and outcomes have different roles | Practice earns XP; finished work opens world stages | “Practice matters. Finished work leaves a mark.” |
| Projects inhabit the world | Prepare, Practice and Deliver connect the project to a region | “A real project becomes an expedition.” |
| Evidence creates keepsakes | Display slots refer to actual confirmed outputs | “Keep something you made.” |
| Absence preserves progress | No decay, damage or required streak multiplier | “Return to the place you left.” |
| Depth appears deliberately | Five main destinations; supporting tools and full paths open on demand | “A clear next step, with a world behind it.” |
| Olive stays optional | Local project guidance and factual reflection; no remote model | “A little company. Your own pace.” |
| Corrections remain first-class | Undo and record changes recalculate the world and eligible wardrobe | “Your records stay yours to refine.” |

These are product distinctions, not verified competitive superiority or evidence of behavior change. Do not claim PROS is the first, best, scientifically proven, therapeutic or guaranteed to build habits.

## Website copy

**Eyebrow:** REAL EFFORT. A LITTLE EVERY DAY.  
**Headline:** Your life. A world of possibility.  
**Body:** Practice a skill. Finish something meaningful. Watch a world grow from the things you do.  
**Primary action:** Open PROS  
**Secondary action:** See how it works

**Section: Give today a clear beginning.** Choose one project and one next action. Log real work in a few fields, or describe it in a sentence and review the suggestions. Focus holds your place when life interrupts.

**Section: Make progress visible.** Restore six illustrated regions through practice and finished results. Keep a recording, a repaired object, a finished book or another observable result in your collection.

**Section: Follow your curiosity.** Explore 74 catalog skills, add your own and adapt their project paths. Levels reflect recorded effort; milestones ask what you can actually show.

**Section: Meet Olive.** A small white Spitz, a quiet companion. Find a smaller next step or reflect on the week with on-device guidance. Your journal is never sent to an AI service for these suggestions.

**Section: Set the atmosphere.** Choose a regional score, a recorded soundtrack or a little forest and water. Sound starts when you choose it. Reduced motion and a quieter focus view are always available.

**Final invitation:** Begin with one thing you care about. There is room to grow from there.

## Press and presentation

**Press boilerplate:** PROS is a personal RPG for real-world skill practice and projects. Its six illustrated regions develop through recorded effort and finished outputs. The app combines project planning, focus sessions, reading, journaling and an evidence-linked Chronicle with optional music, a customizable character and Olive, its white Spitz companion. PROS preserves progress through absences and supports local-first saving, corrections and portable backups.

**30-second spoken pitch:** “PROS gives your real projects a world to live in. Pick a small next action, practice, then keep the thing you finished. Your skills develop and six illustrated regions gradually come back to life. It has the everyday tools I need—focus, projects, books and a journal—with a clear daily opening. Olive is there when I want a smaller step. Taking a break doesn’t erase anything.”

**Five-slide presentation:**

1. The experience: “A world of your making” with the actual Today screen.
2. The loop: choose → practice → confirm a result → restore a place.
3. The distinction: effort, outputs and keepsakes connected without repeated XP.
4. The everyday product: projects, focus, reading, Chronicle and Olive.
5. The invitation: controlled private access, actual installation/support URL and honest availability.

**Media assets:** `public/art/arrival.webp`, `home.webp`, six region paintings, `olive.webp` and `favicon.svg`. Credit generated illustrations as created for PROS with ImageGen; source art/audio attribution remains in the public license files. Olive’s full generation/edit brief is retained. Do not represent concept art or model renders as app screenshots.

Post only where community rules permit, disclose authorship, use actual availability and invite specific feedback. No messages or posts have been sent. Do not include fabricated testimonials, user counts, ratings or retention claims.

## Launch operator checklist

Complete the technical release gates first. Confirm the private account/rules deployment, owner access and actual hosting domain. Finish the paused device/accessibility/performance/audio checks and seven-day owner beta. Then review the support channel, privacy notice, asset credits, screenshots and store availability claims against the deployed build.

Maintain an internal issue/release log in documentation; keep phase names, implementation notes and change-log language out of the normal product interface. Success should be assessed through real completed daily flows, useful returns and absence of data-loss defects, not invented engagement statistics.
