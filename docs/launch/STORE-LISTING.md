## Description

Give real effort a place to live.

PROS is a personal RPG for the things you care about learning and making. Choose a project, practice a skill and keep a real finished result. Your character develops as six illustrated regions gradually come back to life.

A CLEAR PLACE TO BEGIN
Today follows your chosen project and its next action. Log work in a few fields, or describe it in a sentence and review the suggested skill, time and date. Focus keeps its place through interruptions, with confirmation before time becomes XP.

A WORLD THAT REFLECTS YOUR WORK
Restore the Observatory, Foundry, Atelier, Training Grounds, Citadel and Sanctuary. Practice and finished outputs both matter. Display keepsakes linked to things you actually completed, and explore regional chapters at your own pace.

ROOM FOR CURIOSITY
Explore 74 catalog skills, add custom skills and adapt projects to your own interests. Keep reading sessions, book notes, reflections and an evidence-linked Chronicle together.

MEET OLIVE
A small white Spitz offers optional, on-device next-step guidance and factual weekly reflection. No remote AI service receives your journal for these suggestions.

YOUR PACE
Choose music, nature sounds or silence. Use reduced motion and a quieter focus view. Time away never damages your character or removes cumulative progress.

YOUR RECORDS
Review corrections before saving, undo supported changes, and export portable backups including unfinished drafts. Account progress saves locally first and synchronizes privately when connected. Initial account setup requires a connection, email verification and enabled access.

Skill levels represent recorded effort in PROS; they are not professional qualifications. Closed-app timer alarms are not provided.
