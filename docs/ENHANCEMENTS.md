## Today and Focus

Today leads with a pinned project or a place to begin. Keep a short list of priorities below it. **Log activity** credits practice to one skill or a reviewed split between two. The optional sentence field can suggest fields from a line such as `guitar 25m yesterday`. Check the fields and save the entry yourself.

Use **Start focus** or open Focus to choose a countdown or stopwatch. You can pause and return. End a session to review the elapsed time before recording it. A continuation note gives you a place to resume. The phone’s compact focus banner opens a session you left running or paused.

## Quests and Skills

Quests holds your one-off actions, routines, and projects. A project can have preparation steps, a practice target, and a finished result. When you can point to the result, record it. Routine occurrences can be completed, skipped, or moved individually. A checkbox completes a step without adding practice time.

Skills shows the paths you use most. PROS has 110 authored skill paths across six regions. Open **Discover** to add optional paths. You can also make a custom skill, use its path as a starting point, and create a project around the work you actually want to finish.

## World, keepsakes, and Character

Each of the six regions has three restoration stages. Practice time and distinct finished results work together. The first stage takes 60 minutes of relevant practice and one finished result. Later stages ask for more practice and results. The final stage also needs a completed project. **World** shows the current requirements and lets you preview appearances.

A finished result can become a keepsake that you display at home. Character brings together your portrait, wardrobe, attributes, and milestones. Its wayfinder has an optional 3D view with keyboard and button controls and an illustrated fallback. Correcting the underlying work recalculates the affected experience, restoration, and rewards.

## Journal, Library, and Chronicle

Find these tools under **More** on a phone, or in the desktop sidebar. Use Journal for a thought worth keeping and Library for the books and reading sessions you want to track. Chronicle brings practice and results together. Insights shows how your effort was distributed over time.

## Olive, sound, and motion

Olive offers a smaller next step for a project and a factual recap of the week. Her written guide and sentence-based activity capture work locally. The optional local model is a separate download and needs a compatible browser. Review every suggestion before it becomes a draft or a saved record. [Local AI guide](LOCAL-AI.md)

In the soundtrack library, choose music, nature sounds, and cues. Playback starts when you choose it. Optional sound downloads are separate from the core offline app. Choose **System** for motion in Settings to follow your device’s reduced-motion preference. [Art and audio](ART-AND-AUDIO.md)

## Save, recover, and return

Changes save on the device first. A verified account can synchronize them and restore them on another device after sign-in. Recover drafts, export a backup, or review competing edits in **Settings → Data and recovery**. The short guided tour is optional and can be reopened from Help.

There is no streak to lose. Your cumulative work stays available when you take time away. [Data recovery](DATA-RECOVERY.md) · [Repository status](IMPLEMENTATION-CHECKPOINT.md)
